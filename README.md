# 📋 Medical Cost & Risk Profiling Dashboard

Welcome to the **Medical Cost & Risk Profiling** notebook! This interactive system analyzes clinical demographic factors (such as Age, BMI, and Smoking Status) to forecast healthcare charges and classify health risk profiles.

### 🚀 Project Highlights
* **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Clear correlation heatmaps revealing that smoking status is the primary cost driver.
* **Cost Prediction (Linear Regression):** Predicts medical charges with enhanced performance via an interaction feature (`bmi_smoker`).
* **Risk Classification (Logistic Regression):** Flips the objective to classify and predict smoking status with high accuracy.
* **Interactive Predictor & Calculator:** Widgets to dynamically profile individual patients and predict their potential charges.
